# Quick Cheat Sheet for Ctrl + r in Command Mode:
#source

<C-r><C-w> : Grabs the word under the cursor.
<C-r><C-a> : Grabs the WORD (space-separated) under the cursor.
<C-r><C-l> : Grabs the whole line under the cursor.
<C-r><C-f> : Grabs the file name under the cursor.
