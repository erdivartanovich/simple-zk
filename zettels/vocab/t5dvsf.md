# Unequivocally

> Unequivocally means doing or saying something clearly so that it leaves no room for doubt, confusion, or other meanings.

## Meaning and Usage
Clear truth: Stating a fact so it is fully understood as true.
No doubt: Leaving zero space for a second guess or wrong idea.
Firm choice: Giving a direct "yes" or "no" answer.

## Good Examples in Sentences
- "She unequivocally denied the bad rumors."
- "He unequivocally stated that the work was done."
- "The data unequivocally proves the point."
